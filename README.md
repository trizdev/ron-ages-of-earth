# Rise of Animals: Ages of Earth

Rise of Animals: Ages of Earth is a free fan mod for Rise of Nations: Extended Edition (Steam). It is a variant of Rise of Animals where the game's 8 ages become Earth's geological eras, in order, from the Precambrian to the Cenozoic, with English pun names such as The Jurass-kick Age.

The project is in the design phase. No game files have been modified yet.

Design document (Portuguese): [docs/design-doc.md](docs/design-doc.md).

The mod is free and will always be free, with no ads.
