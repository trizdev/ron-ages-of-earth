# Rise of Nations: Ages of Earth

Rise of Nations: Ages of Earth is a free, standalone fan mod for Rise of Nations: Extended Edition (Steam). The 24 nations become evolutionary lineages of prehistoric animals, and the game's 8 ages become Earth's geological eras, in order:

1. The Primordial Age (Precambrian)
2. The Cambrian Explosion
3. The Age of Fins (Devonian)
4. The Coal Forest Age (Carboniferous)
5. The Triassic Dawn
6. The Jurassic Heights
7. The Cretaceous Bloom
8. The Cenozoic Rise

Units evolve with each era, buildings become prehistoric habitats, technologies become evolutionary adaptations, and maps become prehistoric biomes, in a realistic Rise of Nations art style.
